# Enterprise AI Advisor

## Project Overview

Enterprise AI Advisor is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) application that helps IT leaders, engineers, and business users make informed decisions about enterprise AI adoption.

The app answers questions about enterprise AI tools, AI governance, RAG architecture, embeddings, vector databases, LangChain, LangGraph, and tool selection. It retrieves relevant documentation from a curated enterprise AI knowledge base and generates grounded answers with citations.

## One-Line RAG Statement

My RAG app helps IT leaders, engineers, and business teams answer enterprise AI adoption, governance, and architecture questions from a curated enterprise AI knowledge base through a Streamlit chatbot with grounded answers and source citations.

## Use Case

Organizations adopting AI tools often need guidance on:

- Whether to use ChatGPT, Claude, or Microsoft Copilot
- When to use RAG instead of fine-tuning
- How embeddings and vector databases work
- How to govern enterprise AI usage
- How to design internal knowledge assistants

Enterprise AI Advisor acts like a lightweight AI consultant for those questions.
