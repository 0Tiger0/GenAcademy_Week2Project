# ChatGPT Models

## Overview

ChatGPT provides access to multiple AI models optimized for different workloads.

## Model Categories

### General Purpose Models

Used for:

- Writing
- Research
- Analysis
- Coding
- Summarization

### Reasoning Models

Designed for:

- Complex problem solving
- Multi-step reasoning
- Technical analysis
- Decision support

## Enterprise Considerations

Organizations should select models based on:

- Accuracy requirements
- Cost
- Speed
- Complexity of tasks

## Advisor Notes

Reasoning-focused models are often preferred for architecture recommendations and technical decision making.
