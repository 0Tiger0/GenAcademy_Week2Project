# Enterprise AI Governance

## What is Enterprise AI Governance?

Enterprise AI governance is the set of policies, controls, and processes used to manage AI adoption responsibly and securely.

## Why Governance Matters

Without governance, organizations may face risks such as:

- Data leakage
- Inaccurate outputs
- Compliance issues
- Unauthorized AI usage
- Poor decision quality

## Core Governance Areas

### Acceptable Use

Define what employees can and cannot do with AI tools.

### Data Protection

Clarify what data can be entered into AI systems.

### Access Management

Control who has access to enterprise AI tools.

### Human Oversight

Require human review for high-impact decisions.

### Monitoring

Track usage, adoption, risk, and policy compliance.

## Enterprise Controls

Organizations should consider:

- AI usage policy
- Approved tools list
- Security review
- Legal review
- User training
- Audit logging
- Sensitive data guidance

## Advisor Notes

AI governance should not only block risky behavior. It should help employees use approved AI tools safely, effectively, and consistently.
