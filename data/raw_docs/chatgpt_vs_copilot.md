# ChatGPT Enterprise vs Microsoft Copilot

## ChatGPT Enterprise

Strengths:

- General AI assistant
- Research
- Writing
- Coding
- Knowledge assistants
- Custom GPTs

## Microsoft Copilot

Strengths:

- Microsoft 365 integration
- Outlook
- Teams
- Word
- Excel
- PowerPoint

## When to Choose ChatGPT

Organizations often choose ChatGPT when they need a flexible AI platform for multiple business use cases.

## When to Choose Copilot

Organizations often choose Copilot when most business workflows already operate inside Microsoft 365.

## Advisor Notes

Many enterprises deploy both platforms because they solve different business problems.
