# ChatGPT Enterprise Security

ChatGPT Enterprise is designed for organizations that need security, privacy, and administrative controls.

## Data Privacy

OpenAI does not train its models on ChatGPT Enterprise customer prompts or business data.

## Security Controls

Enterprise features may include encryption, admin management, domain verification, SSO, and compliance-focused controls.

## Enterprise Use Cases

Organizations use ChatGPT Enterprise for secure productivity, document analysis, research, coding support, and internal knowledge workflows.

## Advisor Notes

ChatGPT Enterprise is a good fit when organizations need a general-purpose AI assistant with strong privacy and administrative controls.
